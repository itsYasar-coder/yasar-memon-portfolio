About

A little about how I work

I like problems with real constraints: real users, real data, real deadlines.

I'm Yasar, a full stack developer who enjoys the whole arc of a product — modelling the data, designing the API, and then sweating the last ten percent of the interface. I care about fast pages, keyboard access, and pull requests that are easy to review.
